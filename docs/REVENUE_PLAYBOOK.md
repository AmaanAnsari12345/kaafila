# Revenue Playbook

KAAFILA should not depend on a single monetization stream. The strongest model is blended revenue:

- Subscriptions: `KAAFILA PRO`
- Ads: free-plan inventory
- Direct brand sponsorship: highest-margin layer
- Fleet / enterprise contracts
- Event and tourism operator software fees

## Recommended Revenue Mix

- Subscriptions: 40-55%
- Direct brand deals: 25-40%
- Ads: 10-20%
- Fleet / enterprise: 10-25%

These are planning targets, not guarantees.

## Ad Inventory

Free-tier inventory that fits the product without making it feel cheap:

- Convoy lobby banner
- Rewarded unlock for journey replay export
- Route sponsor cards between checkpoints
- Pit stop recommendation sponsor modules
- AI movie end-card sponsor
- Family follow view sponsor tile

Use ads only on free surfaces. Keep live convoy safety workflows free of interruption.

## Brand Inventory

High-value sponsor packages:

1. Title Convoy
   Naming rights, branded room, replay intro, event QR assets.
2. Route Takeover
   Sponsor checkpoint cards, fuel offers, roadside hospitality.
3. Pit Stop Partner
   Food, fuel, EV, washroom, and lounge activations.
4. Safety Sponsor
   SOS workflows, tyre care, rainy route guidance, emergency partner placement.
5. Media Sponsor
   GoPro-style creator packs, replay watermark, highlight reel partner placement.

## Outbound Brand Categories

- Fuel and lubricant brands
- Tyres and auto accessories
- EV charging networks
- Action cameras and creator tools
- Resorts, hotels, and tourism boards
- Wedding and event operators
- Roadside assistance and insurance

## Monetization Stack

- `AdMob` for mobile app monetization and SDK coverage across Android and iOS.
- `Google Ad Manager` when KAAFILA needs direct-sold inventory, app inventory controls, and programmatic orchestration.
- `AppLovin` or another mediation/ad growth partner for mobile scale tests, especially when gaming-style rewarded economics translate well to replay/video unlocks.

## Revenue SOP

1. Launch free + Pro together.
2. Turn on only 2-3 ad surfaces first.
3. Sell 1-2 direct sponsor pilots before scaling broad outbound.
4. Track:
   sponsor fill, sponsor CTR, replay export completion, Pro conversion, churn, convoy completion.
5. Protect trust:
   no intrusive ads during SOS, navigation, or critical safety moments.

## Pricing Starting Point

Use these as early outbound anchors:

- Title convoy: `INR 3L-10L per event`
- Route takeover: `INR 1L-4L per route`
- Pit stop partner: `INR 75K-2L per stop`
- Safety sponsor: `INR 50K-1.5L per month`
- Replay / AI movie sponsor: `INR 40K-2L per campaign`

Actual pricing should be recalibrated after the first 5-10 paid pilots.
